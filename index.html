Application checklist

Qualification reality check

A UK Corporate Law LLM is an academic qualification, not admission as a solicitor. England & Wales qualification generally requires a degree/equivalent, SQE1 and SQE2, two years of qualifying work experience and suitability checks. Official SRA ↗
Training contract / vacation schemeBest if still unqualified; check graduate eligibility and future intake year.
Paralegal / legal assistantPotential entry points into M&A, finance, legal research and due diligence. Some work may count as QWE if properly confirmed.
Junior legal / legal analystReview legal qualification and UAE licensing conditions for any advisory duties.
Associate / legal counselUsually requires admission as a lawyer and experience. Do not assume an LLM alone is sufficient.
Non-UAE-national applicants: UK-firm training programmes may be open internationally, but specific Emirati programmes are reserved for UAE nationals. UAE local-court advocacy is a separate licensing issue. Dubai Legal Affairs Department ↗
